OFFICIAL SYLLABUS ALIGNMENT

Midterm Examination Priority Guide

Directly aligned with Asst. Prof. Kenan Büyükatak's official midterm announcement. Focus on conceptual clarity, IT mechanisms, and real business examples.

Full-Credit 4-Part Exam Response Rubric (20 Points Each)
1. Accurate Definition Explain the concept in your own clear words; avoid shallow keyword recitations.
2. IS Mechanism Describe how data, software, networks, or telematics physically execute the system.
3. Compare / Contrast Distinguish the concept from adjacent models (e.g. TPS vs. MIS, ERP vs. CRM).
4. Real-World Business Anchor the answer in an actual company (e.g. Walmart, Apple, Disney, Ponsse, TELUS).
